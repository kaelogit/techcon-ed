Why this notice was issued

While arranging final delivery documents for Lynn Zakowski’s approved $300,000 support, our office found that no insurance coverage is currently in place on the award. Escort protects the team and the run; it is not the same as insurance on the funds themselves.

What insurance covers
  • In transit: If funds are lost, stolen, damaged, or otherwise compromised on the road during escorted delivery, insurance creates a recovery path. Without it, there is no carrier-backed recovery system for the $300,000 while it is moving.
  • At and after handoff: An award of this size remaining in a private residence without coverage leaves the recipient exposed if something happens after delivery. Insurance is the protection layer on the award itself.
  • Dispatch clearance: Our delivery team cannot take charge of an uninsured $300,000 run. Escort booking alone does not satisfy document clearance when insurance is missing.
The Foundation does not underwrite award insurance in-house. Coverage is placed through an external carrier for the recipient’s file before dispatch. This is separate from the administrative fee and separate from the security escort fee already paid.
